Sunless

Sunless is the main character of the novel called Shadow Slave. He is the epigone of Weaver(Daemon of Fate), the heir of death(The Shadow God), and the treacherous Lost From Light.

History

Sunless, otherwise known as Sunny, hasnt always been the sovereign of the shadows. Before he began ascending the path towards divinity...

  • He lived in the dark streets of NQSC, surviving by stealing, lying, and scavenging.
  • His sister was adopted after his parents died, while he was left to rot.
  • He was infected by a widespread and most times lethal disease, and assuming he might die, he tried coffee for the first time.
  • He also... killed Auros of the Nine, a soldier of the old imperial army who was destined to play one of the largest roles
    in killing the six remaining Gods, and in the meanwhile, he also ended up serving his body up as a sacrifice to Shadow.

Sunless trudged through a dark and eerie road to get to where he is today, and before he started, Fate was rough on him... So, he swore to break it.

Lost From Light

A true name gifted to Sunless from the first child of Weaver, The Nightmare Spell, after conquering his First Nightmare. It represents his connection to fate, and how he will eventually sever that bond tying him down to mortal laws.
